# PRODUCT SCOPE

> Defines what AUSTRO OS is, what it is not, and the boundaries of the product.

---

# Purpose

This document defines the functional and strategic boundaries of AUSTRO OS.

Its purpose is to ensure that every architectural decision, implementation, and future feature aligns with the product vision while preventing unnecessary complexity and scope expansion.

---

# Product Identity

AUSTRO OS is an AI Content Operating System.

It is a platform that enables autonomous AI workforces to plan, execute, review, and improve content production workflows.

It is designed to manage work rather than conversations.

# Out of Scope

The following are intentionally excluded from AUSTRO OS.

## General Purpose Chatbot

AUSTRO OS is not designed to compete with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini as a conversational assistant.

---

## Social Media Platform

The platform does not replace YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or similar services.

---

## Video Editing Software

AUSTRO OS orchestrates editing workflows but is not intended to replace professional editing software.

---

## AI Model Development

The project consumes AI models but does not build foundational language models.

---

## Hardware Management

The platform does not manage servers, operating systems, or physical infrastructure directly.

---

# Design Boundaries

Every new feature must satisfy the following conditions.

- Supports the product vision.
- Improves autonomous execution.
- Fits within the architectural model.
- Can be maintained long term.
- Does not introduce unnecessary complexity.

If a feature does not satisfy these conditions, it must not be added.

---

# Non-Functional Requirements

The platform must prioritize:

- Scalability
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Security
- Privacy
- Extensibility
- Performance
- Observability

---

# Long-Term Product Direction

Future versions may introduce:

- Marketplace
- Plugin Ecosystem
- Multi-Tenant Organizations
- Enterprise Edition
- Self-Improving AI Workforce
- Autonomous Business Optimization

These capabilities must build upon the existing architecture without requiring fundamental redesign.

---

# Scope Governance

Any proposal that expands the product scope must be reviewed against this document before approval.

Changes to this document require architectural review and founder approval.

---

# Conclusion

The scope defined in this document represents the strategic boundaries of AUSTRO OS.

Protecting these boundaries is essential to preserving the platform's long-term quality, maintainability, and vision.
